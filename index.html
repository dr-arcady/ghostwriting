Terms of Service & Agreement

Review the client agreement before creating your order.

1. Scope of Services & Ethics

1.1 Academic Assistance: Research & Composition Assistance is provided as high-level custom model papers, reference drafts, or analytical frameworks to assist independent scholarly work. Clients are solely responsible for compliance with home institution honor codes.

1.2 Creative Writing: Beta reading, developmental editing, poetry critiques, and story consulting represent creative feedback and artistic evaluation. Creative work remains 100% the intellectual property of the author.

2. Intellectual Property & Ownership

2.1 Academic Model Rights: Upon 100% full payment clearance, all rights and title to custom academic deliverables transfer exclusively to the client.

2.2 Creative Rights: For creative writing projects, the author retains 100% ownership, copyright, and publishing rights at all times.

2.3 Privacy Guarantee: Materials will never be shared, published, or trained on public AI databases.

3. Payment Structure & Retainers

Projects require a 50% upfront retainer prior to commencement. The remaining 50% balance is due prior to final watermark-free deliverable release. Large dissertations/manuscripts may utilize a 3-stage milestone model (40/30/30).

4. Revisions & Support Windows

Includes 1 free round of minor adjustments (fixing prose, formatting, or minor adjustments aligned with the original brief) within the active support window (3 to 30 days based on project length).

5. Cancellation & Refunds

Cancellations made after work has commenced forfeit the upfront retainer to cover research and time allocation. Completed work up to the cancellation point will be delivered.
